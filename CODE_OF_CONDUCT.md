# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the work, not the
person.

## What is expected

- Give people the benefit of the doubt, especially across a language barrier.
- Keep disagreements about the thing being built.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no.
- Respect the project's boundaries around rights and consent. This project builds
  tooling for voice cloning, and "how do I clone a voice I have no right to" is
  not a question it will help with.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demographic insults, deliberate misgendering,
sexual attention or imagery, publishing others' private information, or sustained
disruption.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, plus public
spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via
[private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new) or by opening an
issue asking for a contact route. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may ban a contributor
temporarily or permanently for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Where the call
is not clear cut, maintainers will explain their reasoning.
