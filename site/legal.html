Safety and terms.

Read this before you install a voice. Installing one means you agree to it.

Drive first. The voice comes second.

Backseat voices are for fun. A character voice, a joke or a catchphrase can take your attention off the road, and some voices are louder, faster or harder to understand than Waze's standard voice. You are responsible for driving safely.

  • Choose and set up your voice before you drive, never while the car is moving.
  • Don't touch your phone or this site while driving.
  • If a voice distracts you or is hard to follow, switch back to a standard Waze voice.
  • Keep your eyes on the road and obey traffic laws, road signs and the police. A voice saying "turn" never makes a turn safe or legal.
  • Keep the volume at a level where you can still hear sirens, horns and your surroundings.

Directions can be wrong

Directions, alerts and road information come from Waze, not from Backseat. Every voice pack is computer-generated, and a clip may be mispronounced, unclear, or say something slightly different from what Waze meant. Speed camera, police and hazard alerts can be missing, late or wrong. Always use your own judgement.
