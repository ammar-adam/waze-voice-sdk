How to install

  1. 1Get the voice Tap Install in Waze under the voice you want. Scan the voice's QR code with your phone's camera. Waze opens on your phone.
  2. 2Say yesWaze asks if you want to add the voice. Tap Yes.
  3. 3Tap its nameWaze opens Voice & sound for you. Tap the character's name to switch to it. Waze won't pick it for you.

    Add to Waze

    First, open this page in Safari or Chrome. This app's built-in browser can't hand the voice to Waze. Use the ••• menu, then Open in browser.

    1. 1Tap Open Waze below. Waze asks if you want to add the voice. Tap Yes.
    2. 2Tap in the list. Adding a voice doesn't switch to it. The list is in Settings → Voice & sound → Waze voice.
    3. 3 No Waze yet? Get it free from the App Store, open it once, then come back and tap Install again. No Waze yet? Tap Open Waze anyway and install it from Google Play. The voice is waiting when Waze first opens.
