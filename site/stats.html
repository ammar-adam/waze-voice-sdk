    How these are counted

    • Install taps are taps on “Install in Waze” on a phone. Waze takes it from there and doesn't report back, so a tap is an install started, not one Waze has confirmed.
    • *Confirmed installs are people who answered “Yes” to the “Did it work?” prompt after coming back from Waze. They're self-reported, and plenty of people never answer, so the real number is higher.
    • Visitors are estimated without cookies: a one-way hash of the connection, counted in a structure that can't be turned back into who anyone is. No IP address is stored.
    • Character clicks are preview plays plus taps on a face at the top of the page. Known bots aren't counted.
