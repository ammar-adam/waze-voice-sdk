# Demo Video Plan

Goal: show a custom navigation voice working on a real device without distributing restricted assets.

## Constraints

- Keep the demo under 60 seconds.
- Do not publish downloadable copyrighted audio.
- Do not imply official affiliation with Waze, Google, or any rights holder.
- Link to the generic repo, not a restricted voice pack.

## Shot List

1. Phone showing Waze using a custom voice.
2. One clear turn instruction.
3. One route correction or arrival moment.
4. Brief end card pointing to the generic tooling repo.
